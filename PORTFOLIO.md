# Life Tracker

A private, all-in-one personal life app that brings health tracking, a journal, and personal finance into one calm place. Anyone can create an account, and every account only ever sees its own data.

---

## Health

- Log weight, water, food and drink, blood pressure, and daily habits for any day
- Quick-add buttons for your own water bottles and glasses
- Blood pressure readings with time, arm, and posture, as many per day as you need
- Personal ideal ranges, so values above or below your targets are gently flagged
- Averages, trend charts with ideal guide lines, and good and bad habit heatmaps
- Calories and sodium estimated by AI when you don't feel like looking them up
- A shared food and drink database that suggests items as you type
- A place to keep medical information such as allergies and medications

## Journal

- Notes with rich text, checklists, and links, browsable by date or on a calendar
- Turn any note into a to-do in one tap
- Recurring routines (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly) with subtasks; monthly and yearly ones carry over until they're done
- A reminder card on the home page for tasks that are waiting
- To-dos with lists, an archive, priorities, due dates, search, and filters
- A drag-and-drop board that shows what's upcoming, to do, in progress, and done

## Finance

- Accounts for cash, banks, e-wallets, savings, credit cards, investments, and loans, each with its own color, icon, and provider
- Reorder accounts by dragging them, with a lock to keep the order in place
- Record expenses, income, and transfers with categories and labels, and edit or delete them later
- See how many months your money would last at your current spending
- Analytics for cash flow, net over time, spending by category and account, top expenses, and savings rate
- Balances that keep themselves accurate automatically

## Sharing and AI

- Downloadable PDF health reports with summaries, daily tables, and trend charts
- Read-only share links for family or a doctor, with optional expiry and view limits
- Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or other AI assistants to ask questions about your own data

## Made for everyday use

- Works offline and syncs when you're back online
- Installable on your phone like a regular app
- Choose your units for weight, water, and height
- Light and dark mode
